Dear Fasya,

✦

I wrote this a few times before getting to this version. The earlier ones were too careful. This one is honest.

Three years ago you said yes without knowing everything yet. And since then you've just — kept showing up.

Driving me to the airport for Jakarta. Making the trip from KL for my graduation. Running 12km beside me in January like that was always something we did.

So here it is. Written down, and meant.
